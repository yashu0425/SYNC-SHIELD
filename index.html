01 · Decision logic

Three connected actions

1. Relieve 4G anchoring

Steer eligible LTE-only traffic to compatible carriers and the less-loaded M6 footprint. Provide local LTE anchors through 23 proposed dual-RAT small cells.

2. Add targeted radio capacity

34 small cells for stands, food queues and internal gates; 2 temporary COWs at entry/parking/exit; targeted M4/M6 n78 upgrades after backhaul verification.

3. Protect service & mobility

Monitor RRC SR, EN-DC addition SR and PS Drop; test IMS emergency priority, merchant POS fallback, uplink performance and handover tuning.
