Model methodology and limits

Why this is a decision twin, not a calibrated RF twin

Site longitude, latitude, antenna azimuth, heights, beam patterns, handset distribution, neighbor relations, frequency allocation by band, scheduler measurements, and RF channel maps are not provided. Accordingly, the stadium geometry and heatmap are explicitly illustrative. The tool does not predict RSRP, SINR, interference, RRC failure causes or real UPI payment failure rates.

Demand model

active_airtel = crowd × 0.40 × concurrency × phase_multiplier
nr_capable = active_airtel × 0.70
active_on_nr = nr_capable × nr_served_fraction
active_on_lte = active_airtel − active_on_nr
NR_demand_Mbps = active_on_nr × 5
LTE_demand_Mbps = active_on_lte × 3

In the case's full-demand calculation (also used by the final PPT), all 5G-capable active users are treated as receiving NR service. The NR-served percentage in this simulator is not equivalent to the observed 5G connection success percentage, which likely concerns connection attempts.

Equivalent cell/sector capacity — uncertain baseline

NR_sector_planned_Mbps = 100 MHz × NR_eff_bpsHz × 0.80 × (1−loss)
LTE_sector_planned_Mbps = chosen_LTE_MHz × LTE_eff_bpsHz × 0.80 × (1−loss)
NR_sectors = baseline_NR + new_small_cells + chosen_COW_sectors×COW + 3×NR_macro_upgrades
LTE_sectors = baseline_LTE + dual_RAT_small_cells + chosen_COW_sectors×COW

These counts are assumptions. Bandwidth per LTE sector, NR/TDD downlink allocation, MIMO, beam performance, hardware capabilities and headroom need vendor and field verification. Efficiency is modelled as an effective sector-level quantity to avoid duplicate overhead factors.

Microwave bottleneck

M4 and M6 have 2 Gbps microwave backhaul. Without a verified transport upgrade, we cap each upgraded site's combined planned LTE+NR throughput at 80% of 2 Gbps = 1.6 Gbps, assigning LTE load-bearing capacity first and derating that site's NR contribution. This is a conservative site-level model, not a packet simulation.

The quoted small-cell/COW prices do not explicitly guarantee dual-RAT operation or the selected COW sector configurations, integration, power or backhaul. No actual fibre route survey exists. "Arithmetic-feasible" is not a certification of coverage or budget feasibility.

Phase modelling

Measured phases are the previous match's crowd buildup, innings break, final over and post-match. The phase multipliers used for the hypothetical final are illustrative: 0.64, 1.00, 1.00, 0.82. These timing multipliers are hypothetical and do not exceed the stated 18% peak concurrency at default settings. Observed UPI failures remain historical, not predictions of the proposed network.
