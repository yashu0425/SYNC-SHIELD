02 · What we change

Connect → Stay connected → Protect users

Three engineering actions, each with its own acceptance KPIs.

1

Connect to 5G

Investigate LTE anchor accessibility and NR addition. Test eNodeB NR B1 threshold, hysteresis, TTT, and X2 / neighbour configuration.

Measure: RRC setup SR · EN-DC addition SR
2

Stay connected

Test LTE A3/A5, CIO and mobility settings; monitor packet-service drops, secondary-cell failures and unwanted handovers.

Measure: PS Drop · SCG failure · HO SR
3

Protect critical services

Verify per-cell DL/UL PRB below 80%, payment connectivity at food stalls and supported IMS emergency calling.

Measure: PRB load · UPI · emergency tests

NSA note: EN-DC secondary-node addition is not interchangeable with a conventional 4G→5G IRAT handover. Track IRAT HOSR only where an actual IRAT procedure exists.
